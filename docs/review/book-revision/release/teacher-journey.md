# Two-lesson review and teacher journey

This guide matches the book integrated through 6B at `d1fdae5`. It is a facilitator's route
through existing content, not classroom validation or scientific approval. The
[review packet](README.md) links the scientific drafts and limitations.

Start `pnpm dev` from the repository root and open the local URL printed by Vite.
The default route opens **From V to U**. Direct entry to lesson 2 uses
`?spread=tectonics.plate-boundaries`; the research example uses `?view=research`.
Use Contents to navigate without typing a URL.

## From V to U

1. Let the drawing become the scene. Open the answer flap immediately, or leave it
   closed. Do not require a prediction before play.
2. Choose a rock on the page; the specimen-drop gesture is an alternative. Press Play,
   then Pause. Move the timeline to an earlier recorded moment and back to the live end.
3. Choose another rock to start a separate run. The first is kept; compare its labelled
   ghost at the indicated model time. If a matching record is not yet available, follow
   the page's status rather than imply that two unmatched moments are equivalent.
4. Inspect the front face and the under-ice view. These inspect the same run. Switch
   reading level and open the deeper explanation, teacher notes or sources; the model
   state is preserved.
5. Turn to **Where plates meet**. On return, check the selected rock and kept run.
   A run that was playing resumes according to its retained play intent; the time spent
   on the other lesson must not be described as hidden model evolution.

Scientific interpretation and its qualifications are in the
[glacial teacher notes](../../../../uplift/spreads/geomorph.u-valley/teacher-notes.md).
Keep compressed model time distinct from a real valley age.

## Where plates meet

1. Read the illustration label. Choose convergent and use Play or the stage slider.
   Pause at a stage and inspect the front face.
2. Choose divergent, then transform, from the picker or a plate arrow. Each choice
   returns to the shared starting landscape. Compare the labelled ghosts for boundaries
   already viewed at the same stage.
3. Open the answer flap without answering. Switch reading level; inspect the sources
   and the fuller "What this shows / ignores" disclosure.
4. Turn back to **From V to U**, then return using Contents or the page-turn control.
   Keyboard page navigation works from the heading; focused controls retain their
   own keys so a scrubber or input does not accidentally turn the book.

This is an authored illustration, with an early-to-late stage and no simulation clock.
Use the [plate teacher notes](../../../../uplift/spreads/tectonics.plate-boundaries/teacher-notes.md)
for the existing scientific draft and its scope. Do not narrate the drawing as a
physical model generating its relief.

## Optional research stop

Use Contents → Research example. Reveal the explanation without a prediction, then
inspect the recorded rain comparison. A prediction is optional and endpoint feedback
only refers to an optional choice actually committed. The separate live sandbox does not alter the
recorded bars or traces. Return to the book when finished.

The [research teacher notes](../../../../uplift/spreads/geomorph.v-valley/teacher-notes.md)
and [evidence packet](../../../science/validation/rain-comparison/README.md) distinguish
gross material lifted from net incision or export. Failed morphology and the unvalidated
waterfall challenge stay explicit. Do not turn on local collection to run this review.

## When a fallback is shown

Follow the page's actual label. **Showing recorded runs** offers existing captures rather than a new
simulation; the chosen rock, time and comparison survive page turns, and recorded playback pauses
while away. If recordings cannot load, **Illustration preview only** is not scientific process evidence.
**Drawing only** means 3D is unavailable: keep reading, reveal the answer or turn the page; unavailable
scene controls are disabled. These paths are checked on the default app in the
[6B audit](../accessibility/README.md).

For a wide equation in the university reading panel, Tab reaches the equation and arrow keys scroll
it. Escape closes the panel and returns focus to its trigger. These mechanical checks do not replace
actual screen-reader listening.

## Recording and accessible alternatives

The existing [7G short journey recording](../../plates/acceptance/captures/journey-turn-transform.webm)
shows the actual production book, page turn and plate sequence. It is existing 7G evidence,
not a new 6C recording, and does not demonstrate the complete glacier comparison or human
screen-reader use. The [5N captures](../../glacial/acceptance/README.md) and acceptance
journey supplement that scope; the complete route above can be followed live.

Choose controls by keyboard, retain the text alternative for each visual change and try
reduced motion. Phone/reflow layouts and a drawing-only renderer fallback are different
conditions; use the page's actual status. A diagram, video or automated accessibility
scan cannot establish projector legibility or a listener's comprehension. Record those
separately with the setup and observed result in [review records](review-records.md).

Do not coach a first-time reader through this sequence before observing discoverability.
The uncoached observation protocol is separate from this teacher demonstration.

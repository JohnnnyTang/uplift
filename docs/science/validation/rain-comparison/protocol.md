# Rain comparison: prospective presentation protocol

Registered before generating or inspecting the new classroom view. This is a
presentation validation of the complete existing mechanism-isolation rain cohort,
not a new physics experiment or an independent replication of its findings.

- Claim: in these matched exposed-rock runs, heavier rain produces more cumulative
  gross erosion (material lifted from the surface). This is neither net incision
  nor sediment export; repeated erosion after deposition counts again.
- Source: every revised rain-low/rain-high run in the follow-up packet, seeds 1,
  17 and 101, all 111 samples from step 0 through 22000. Keep the recorded 128²
  grid, 10 m cells, 0.05 s fixed timestep, 5 versus 20 mm/h rain and all other
  settings unchanged. No new simulation runs, selected endpoints or tuning.
- Data: mechanically extract the cumulative erodedM3 counter. Check identical
  within-pair initial state hashes, equal configs except rain, matching sample
  steps, complete horizons, finite/nonnegative stocks and both conservation
  checks. Preserve the raw source paths, hashes and solver revision.
- Direction: heavier rain must exceed lighter rain at each of the final three
  samples on every seed, beyond floating-point roundoff. If not, do not score.
- View: two labelled horizontal ochre bars with a common zero and one fixed
  maximum across all frames and seeds. A paired time trace uses the same shared
  axes, with solid/dashed ink lines. No independent normalization or invented
  terrain movement. Every frame is an actual recorded sample; no interpolation.
- Interaction: commit a prediction, then replay the samples over 20 seconds;
  pause, keyboard scrub, inspect the endpoint and replay every seed. Reduced
  motion starts paused. Reading-level changes preserve the replay and live model.
- Visible gate: at 1366×900 and 390×844, endpoint bars and labels must fit without
  horizontal overflow. The longer bar must exceed the shorter by at least 40 CSS
  pixels (a task legibility target, not a scientific threshold). Inspect screenshots
  for all seeds; save a normal-speed recording and accessible bound text results.
- Lesson: ask which rain input lifts more material over the recorded experiment;
  choices lighter/heavier/same. Score only after viewing the endpoint, explicitly
  scope feedback to this comparison, and keep the waterfall challenge pending.

The live terrain below remains free exploration, separately labelled; its controls
do not alter the recorded experiment. The comparison clock reports model steps
and hydraulic seconds, never geological years. Expert scientific review and human
classroom testing remain Stage 1 release requirements; this protocol cannot claim
either. Historical failed morphology gates and calibration records stay intact.

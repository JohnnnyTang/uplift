# Where plates meet: lesson claim/source register (session 7E)

The lesson content lives in
[`uplift/spreads/tectonics.plate-boundaries/`](../../../../uplift/spreads/tectonics.plate-boundaries/README.md).
This note is the human-readable register: **every reader sentence, the claims it rests on and their
sources.** The claims are session 7A's register ([claims.md](../claims.md), PC1–PC20 and NT1–NT7), with
7E's additions below; the machine-readable copy is `claims.ts`. `lesson.test.ts` rebuilds the sentence
table from the copy (`register.ts`) and fails if any row below is missing or stale, so the register cannot
drift from the text. Keys resolve in [sources.md](../sources.md) (what 7A read) and the spread's
`sources.md` (limits of use).

**The rule above every sentence (D-L2-1).** The figure is an illustration. No sentence says or implies that
a landform forms, grows, is computed or is simulated in the scene. Real processes are described as
happening in the world; the scene is described as showing their shapes. No sentence uses "run",
"simulation" or "model time", and no page field carries a year or a typed number.

Only cited real-world figures are typed, only in the deeper level and the teacher notes, and only in a
paragraph whose annotation names the figure's claim (`citedFigures` in `claims.ts`). Everything the
illustration configures is bound: the vertical exaggeration and crust scale (PC20), the Airy ratio and
densities (PC9), the snowline and the block width.
